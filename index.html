About

I study how sequence models remember — and how to make that memory cheaper without changing what the model does.

I'm an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence Engineering in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University. Before CMU, I received a B.A. in Computer Science, with minors in Mathematics and Economics, from New York University.

My research sits between architecture and inference: efficient attention, linear recurrent and state-space models, and the key–value caches that long-context language models depend on — when they can be reused, compressed or restructured while keeping the model's behavior intact. I started out in AI theory and have moved toward applied, measurable systems work. I write my own code end to end and release the code and data behind my papers.

Alongside language models, I collaborate on machine-learning-enhanced multi-criteria decision models for transport safety, published in Scientific Reports, Results in Engineering and Knowledge-Based Systems.

AACL Findings2026

SAFE-KV: Causal Suffix Repair for Behavior-Preserving Non-Prefix KV Cache Reuse in RAG

Xingjian Zhang

Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: AACL-IJCNLP 2026 · to appear

Treats non-prefix KV-cache reuse in retrieval-augmented generation as a behavior-preservation problem — full prefill is the reference and answers are compared by normalized short-answer equivalence — and repairs the causal suffix so that reused caches keep the model's outputs.

Tracing LLM Reasoning Processes with Strategic Games: A Framework for Planning, Revision, and Resource-Constrained Decision Making

Xiaopeng Yuan, Xingjian Zhang, Ke Xu, Yifan Xu, Lijun Yu, Jindong Wang, Yushun Dong, Haohan Wang

AdvGameBench: strategic games (tower defense, auto-battler, turn-based) that expose how LLMs plan, revise and act under resource budgets, with process metrics such as over-correction risk, correction success and over-budget rate related to win rate.

Research collaborations

  1. Efficient Sequence Modeling

    May 2025 – Jan 2026

    With Prof. Eunsol Choi and Yilun Kuang (NYU)

    • Designed SubMLR, a multi-level low-rank attention architecture between quadratic attention and constant-memory linear RNNs; it outperformed strong baselines on Zoology's MQAR task.
    • Built hierarchical chunk-compression models on linear attention (about 10% higher accuracy on synthetic tasks at matched FLOPs) and a FLOPs-matched training and evaluation pipeline on Slurm with PyTorch, Triton and flash-linear-attention.

  2. LLM Reasoning Benchmarks

    Feb 2025 – Jun 2025

    Remote collaboration with Prof. Haohan Wang (UIUC)

    • Co-developed AdvGameBench, a strategic-game benchmark of how LLMs plan and revise under resource budgets, and defined its process metrics.

  3. Chart Question Answering

    May 2025 – Jan 2026

    Remote collaboration with Prof. Qingyun Wang (William & Mary)

    • Extended ChartMoE (InternLM-XComposer2-VL-7B) with nine chart-type experts and built a self-verified Program-of-Thought ChartQA set for SFT and DPO/GRPO.

  4. Transport Safety Modeling

    Jan 2024 – Sep 2025

    Remote collaboration with Dr. Faan Chen (Harvard)

    • Built hybrid clustering and multi-criteria decision models for multi-country road-safety assessment, leading to four journal papers (two first-author) and four open reproduction packages.
